## What is granted

The Grantor grants the Grantee a licence to:

1. **Record and retain** voice recordings and video footage of the Grantor for
   the purpose of building synthetic voice and avatar models.
2. **Create synthetic models** — a cloned voice (ElevenLabs) and a talking-head
   avatar (SadTalker or an equivalent engine) derived from those recordings.
3. **Generate and publish** synthetic speech and video in the Grantor's voice
   and likeness on the channels listed above, including monetised placements.
4. **Retain and re-render** published material after this consent ends, for
   material already published at that time.

Territory: worldwide. Term: from the date signed until withdrawn under
"Withdrawal" below.

## What is not granted

The following are outside this consent and each needs separate written
agreement:

- Use on any channel or platform not listed above.
- Licensing, selling, or transferring the voice or avatar models to a third
  party.
- Political, religious-authority, medical, financial-advice, or endorsement
  content in which the Grantor appears to personally advise or endorse.
- Any statement attributed to the Grantor as their personal opinion that they
  have not reviewed.
- Content that would place the Grantor's likeness in a sexual, defamatory, or
  deceptive context, including presenting synthetic footage as unedited
  documentary record of real events.

## Disclosure

Generated content will be labelled as synthetic where the platform requires it
(YouTube's altered-content disclosure) and wherever a viewer could otherwise
reasonably believe they are watching an unmodified recording.

## Editorial control

The Grantor's voice is used to narrate scripts the Grantor has not individually
approved — that is the point of the pipeline. The safeguard is that factual
claims from the corpus are approved at the inventory level before any script
can use them, and no unverified claim can be published. The Grantor may veto
any individual published item at any time, and the Grantee will unpublish it
within 72 hours of a written request.

## Withdrawal

The Grantor may withdraw this consent at any time by written notice. On
withdrawal:

- No new synthetic content will be generated after the notice date.
- The voice and avatar models will be deleted within 30 days.
- Material already published may remain published unless the Grantor
  specifically requests removal of identified items.

## Data handling

Source recordings and derived models are stored on the Grantee's server with
access restricted to the operator. Voice model identifiers and API credentials
are stored as 0600 files and never committed to a repository or shared in
chat.
